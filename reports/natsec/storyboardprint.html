The reading

The storyboard is the sequence as placed: every workstream on the record with its horizon, its launch and completion months, what it depends on and the people, process and technology it takes. It is the transformation plan's source — the plan reads it and draws it — and it describes and sequences without gating anything.

Fifty-five workstreams are placed: twelve in the transition window, twenty-four in the first horizon, eighteen in the second and one in the third; thirty carry a production gate. The assessment's moves sit in the same horizons — zero, nineteen, twenty-four and eight — so each horizon's table below names the positions its workstreams lift.

Production gates by domain

DOMAINWHAT MUST BE CREDIBLE BEFORE WORKLOADS LEAVE THE SANDBOX
Governance & mission ownershipGovernance is the authority gate: the AO relationship, risk tolerance, and decision forum must exist before any authorization package can be sponsored.
Platform / landing zoneThe landing zone is the primary production gate: no workload leaves sandbox for an authorized mission-cloud environment until the accredited platform boundary, tenancy, identity, and baseline exist to receive it.
Operations / observabilityOperations is the sustainment gate: the AO expects credible Day-2 evidence before production, and ConMon consumes its telemetry from day one.
ITIL service practicesService practice is the throughput gate: manual change boards become the binding constraint on deployment frequency once pipelines mature.
DevSecOps / IaC / CaCThe toolchain is the evidence-velocity gate: it determines whether score-4 (automated, reusable) evidence is achievable anywhere else.
Authorization evidence (RMF)Authorization is a client/AO decision gate: IATT may allow bounded testing, operational use requires the applicable authorization, and cATO is a later continuous-evidence posture rather than a scheduled release event.
Security control inheritanceInheritance is the package-scoping gate: what the platform provides determines what each workload package must still prove.
Application dependency visibilityVisibility is the scoping gate: wave grouping, boundary design, and package scoping all consume the dependency map.
Migration execution readinessExecution is the cutover gate: no wave moves without readiness criteria, a rollback path, and authorization carried for the migrated system.
FinOps / service catalogFinOps is the sustainability gate: consumption funding, tagging, and accountability determine whether the platform survives its second budget year.

Timing values are planning scaffolds on the 0–48-month horizon model, not delivery commitments. Evidence informs; named individuals with authority decide. The applicable Authorizing Official retains every authorization decision.
