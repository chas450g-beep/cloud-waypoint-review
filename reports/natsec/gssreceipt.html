The reading

This receipt closes the GSS design: the boundary drawn, the system categorized, inheritance mapped and the nine decisions taken with owners — the hard first half of a general support system's security plan. It is a foundation, not an SSP draft and not an authorization outcome; the applicable Authorizing Official retains every authorization decision.

The stack rests on AWS GovCloud (US) — USAF enterprise IL5 mission-cloud landing zone (fictional), operated by USAF enterprise IL5 mission-cloud operator (fictional) at IL5 and authorized by USAF enterprise Authorizing Official (fictional), with four of its six landing-zone documents verified; five tenants sit on it. The boundary holds eleven components and six flows, four of them crossing; the system is categorized C:M I:M A:M.

Of the nine decisions, nine are decided; the instruments behind nine are ready. Each is named in the ring below and tabled in the appendix with its owner, date and rationale.
