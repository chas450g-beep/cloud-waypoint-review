WORKSTREAM 01 · GSS FOUNDATIONS

Design the environment applications will enter.

Foundations turns mission knowledge, existing artifacts, and stakeholder decisions into a proposed design for the General Support System—before or alongside landing-zone delivery.

APPROXIMATELY SIX WEEKS

A foundation milestone inside a larger readiness journey.

This workstream establishes what the future GSS is expected to contain, support, secure, and operate. It can start early and then continue to mature as observed dependency evidence becomes available. It does not promise or replace an ATO.

THE MISSION VIEW

What the mission says must be true.

01

Boundary & ownership

Define candidate GSS components, external dependencies, provider responsibilities, control ownership, and decision authority.

02

Shared services

Identify identity, network, logging, monitoring, data, platform, and security services applications will rely upon.

03

Target patterns

Describe repeatable landing, connectivity, migration, and operating patterns without prematurely locking implementation.

04

Authorization inputs

Organize proposed boundaries, data flows, available inherited controls, application-specific responsibilities, evidence gaps, and open decisions for the responsible authorization teams.

05

Migration assumptions

Document current constraints, likely migration paths, sequencing conditions, and what requires observed validation.

06

Operating model

Establish future roles, provider handoffs, service ownership, change paths, continuous monitoring, and skills implications.

THE HANDOFF

Foundation hypotheses become testable.

Dependency evidence can confirm or challenge the declared architecture. Landing-zone and GSS responsibilities may overlap; the proposed authorization boundary and control responsibilities must be agreed with the responsible owners and authorizing official.
