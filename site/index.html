EIGHT WEEKS, THEN EIGHTEEN · IL5 AND IL6

In eight weeks, a foundation the mission can sign. In eighteen to twenty, one it can verify.

Foundations delivers a signed design from what the mission attests. Dependency mapping, recommended alongside it, verifies that design against what the estate actually does. Both figures cover two environments, IL5 and IL6.

Foundations · ~8 weeks · attested evidence

Works from what the mission attests: the data call, the interviews, the documents and plans it already holds. It can run alone.

Dependency mapping · 12 weeks minimum · observed, recommended

Observed application flows on top of the attested evidence, overlapping Foundations from its first weeks. This is where migration wave patterns and groupings become visible, and the same artifacts jump-start a Zero Trust follow-on.

Landing-zone ATO · 24–28 weeks · the provider’s clock

The landing zone’s own authorization is the provider’s and JWCC’s work stream, not this engagement’s. The readiness work runs inside it.

Before migration exposes the gaps, Cloud Waypoint brings together what applications still need:

  • A secure operating environment
  • Dependencies that are understood
  • An authorization path
  • Migration sequencing
  • A team prepared to run it
  • All of it during the landing zone's authorization, not after

One practitioner, consistent from the first conversation to handover. The practice speaks in its own name; the studios are its instruments.

THE SHAPE OF THE RUN

Two streams of evidence, overlapping into one design.

Attested evidence from Foundations; observed flows from dependency mapping; one design from both. The journey unpacks it; what you leave with marks the Zero Trust deliverables.

ONE PROGRAM · CONNECTED WORKSTREAMS

Six workstreams, one system of readiness.

01

Orient

Choose the cloud and operating path around mission outcomes, constraints, and current investments. Start

02

Establish

Establish the proposed foundation: GSS boundary, shared services, target patterns, evidence needs, and ownership. Foundations

03

Observe

Map how applications actually communicate so migration and security decisions begin with evidence. Dependency & Launch

04

Converge

Turn declared intent and observed behavior into migration waves, target data flows, communities of interest, and ATO inputs. How the evidence converges

05

Launch

Prepare the first workload and its application-level authorization evidence for the environment it will enter. What comes after

06

Operate

Make day-two roles, continuous monitoring, service management, and change ownership explicit. Operate

WHAT WAITS, AND WHAT ONLY LOOKS LIKE IT DOES

Most of the work needs no letter.

Green work starts the day you decide to start; three steps genuinely wait for the landing-zone letter. Choose a letter month on the journey and watch what moves.

STARTS NOW

Needs no letter

  • Categorize and select controls
  • Confirm inherited mission services
  • Define the application shape
  • Map mission users to ICAM groups
  • Plan the dual run and the fail-back
  • Draft the mission service catalog
WAITS

Genuinely waits for the letter

  • Vend the workload account to the test OU
  • Place the ZTNA connector in the IL VPC
  • Establish tester access to IL5
BEFORE THE FIRST APPLICATION MOVES

Know what needs to connect—and what should be restricted.

  • What the mission owns and what providers deliver
  • Which applications and services depend on one another
  • How migration waves should be grouped and sequenced
  • Which connections need access, tighter controls, or further review
  • What evidence can support platform and workload authorization
  • Who operates, monitors, changes, and sustains the GSS
