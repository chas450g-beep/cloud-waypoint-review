THE OPERATING REALITY

Every shared service needs clear operating ownership.

Providers, mission teams, and application owners may share operating responsibilities. Cloud Waypoint makes those arrangements explicit early: who monitors, approves changes, responds to incidents, maintains evidence, and reviews access as mission needs evolve.

THE DAY-TWO MODEL

Six responsibilities that need an owner.

01

Service ownership

Maintain the GSS service catalog, performance expectations, dependencies, and accountable service owners.

02

Security operations

Coordinate logging, alerting, vulnerability response, Zero Trust policy, and boundary protection.

03

Continuous monitoring

Collect and curate evidence, track control health, manage findings, and support ongoing authorization decisions.

04

Change & configuration

Define who approves, implements, tests, and records platform, workload, and access-policy changes, including exceptions and rollback.

05

Incident & continuity

Define provider-to-mission escalation, recovery responsibilities, communications, and mission continuity decisions.

06

Consumption & skills

Make cost, entitlement, vendor coordination, staffing, and training part of the operating design.
