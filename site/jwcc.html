VEHICLE-AGNOSTIC BY DESIGN

Cloud Waypoint does not compete with the landing zone.

It helps the mission become a prepared consumer of the environment and services delivered through JWCC, a cloud provider, or an implementation partner. The work focuses on the connective tissue that otherwise emerges late: boundaries, dependencies, evidence, migration patterns, ownership, and operations.

BOUNDARIES MATTER

Cloud Waypoint prepares decisions and evidence. It does not grant authorization.

Authorization scope and decisions remain with the responsible authorities. Workloads may be included within a hosting boundary or require separate authorization. Shared services and multiple clouds do not automatically create one umbrella ATO.
