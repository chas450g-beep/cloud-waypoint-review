What you leave with

Fifteen deliverables. One record you keep.

Not fifteen documents kept in step by hand. One record, printed at the depth each reader needs. Every print says what it still lacks before it is complete, rather than printing a blank as if it were an answer.

And the handoff

To the team that implements, in the files their systems take.

The implementation handoff carries boundaries, dependencies, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria to your chosen team. The record exports an OSCAL system security plan and plan of action, and eMASS-shaped files, so nothing is retyped into your system of record. Exports refuse rather than invent.

What it is not

Not an ATO. Not an implementation. Not a migration.

No score is a gate. No recommendation becomes an authorization. Prior work you bring is frozen as provenance and never re-scored. The Authorizing Official keeps every authorization decision, and every print says so.
