NOT FIFTEEN DOCUMENTS

One record, printed at the depth each reader needs.

Not fifteen documents kept in step by hand. Each deliverable is a projection of the same record, so a correction made once appears everywhere it belongs—and every print says what it still lacks before it is complete, rather than printing a blank as if it were an answer.

THREE DEPTHS OF ONE CONVERSATION

Who reads what.

The sponsor, the program and the engineering team receive the same mission story, not three disconnected documents. Every conclusion shows what supports it, how certain it is, and who still has to decide.

For the sponsor

What leadership decides on.

  • Executive brief What leadership needs to understand and decide.
  • Foundations receipt The entry: the moment, the thesis, who signs.
  • Delivery note What was handed over, and what was accepted.
  • Closeout receipt The record sealed by digest; the pack.

For the program

What the program runs on.

  • Transformation storyboard Workstreams on four horizons, sized and gated.

WHERE ZERO TRUST LIVES

Six of the fifteen carry the pre-plan. None is added for it.

The transformation plan reads what moves, and in what order, from the dependency map. The concept of operations names who runs the platform that enforces each segment on day two. The authorization readiness receipt and the GSS exit receipt carry the boundary, the inheritance and every crossing. The system security plan draft assembles boundary statements rather than inventing them, and the plan of action turns every undeclared crossing into a finding with an owner and a clock.

HOW EVERY PRINT BEHAVES

Derived, never asserted.

01

Read, not typed

Every print is a projection of the record. Edit the record and the print follows.

02

Complete or not yet

Each print carries its own completion contract and names what it still lacks.

03

Marked and sealed

Cover and marking on every print; receipts sealed with the record’s digest.

AND THE HANDOFF

To the team that implements.

The implementation handoff carries boundaries, dependencies, responsibilities and acceptance criteria to your chosen team. The system security plan and plan of action arrive as drafts assembled from the record—refused rather than invented wherever the record cannot support a statement. The record itself is what the mission keeps using after handover.

WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER

No score becomes acceptance.

No recommendation becomes an authorization, no completed stage supplies funding, procurement, release or deployment authority, and no print decides anything. Prior work you bring is frozen as provenance and never re-scored. The authorizing official keeps every authorization decision.
