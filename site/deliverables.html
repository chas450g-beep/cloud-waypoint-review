Midpoint snapshotConfirm the starting position while there is time to adjust.

For sponsors, engineers and cybersecurity leads. Foundations receipt and assessment: the mission context, the evidence position and the questions still to resolve.

Readiness packageBring design and evidence into a reviewable package.

For the program, engineering team and authorizing chain. GSS exit receipt, package readiness view, draft system security plan, plan of action and milestones, and concept of operations.

These are inputs to RMF and authorization preparation. Formal control assessment and the authorizing official’s decision remain with the designated parties. Content reflects the record and agreed scope; unresolved evidence and control responsibilities stay visible.

Transformation packageMake sequence, dependencies and next decisions clear.

For program leadership and receiving delivery teams. Transformation plan, the road ahead and executive brief. Application modernization choices, dependencies and transition priorities inform the sequence. These organize proposed work and decisions; delivery commitments are agreed separately.

Final package — FoundationsRecord acceptance and carry forward the open work.

For sponsors and accountable owners. Foundations closeout receipt documenting handover, recorded acceptance and outstanding work. The combined program continues to its agreed 18-week completion and acceptance milestones.
