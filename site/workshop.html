THE PURPOSE

Leave with a bounded next move.

The workshop is not a compressed assessment. It surfaces the decisions, evidence gaps, access constraints, and stakeholder responsibilities needed to scope the right work—whether that begins with Foundations, dependency mapping, or a coordinated program.

THREE HOURS · FOUR CONVERSATIONS

A practical agenda.

  1. 00–30

    Mission & outcomes

    Define the operational outcome, urgency, decision authority, and what success must enable.

  2. 30–75

    Cloud & boundary

    Review landing-zone status, classification boundaries, shared services, authorization posture, and ownership assumptions.

  3. 75–135

    Applications & evidence

    Examine inventories, application knowledge, telemetry, documentation, known dependencies, and blind spots.

  4. 135–180

    Sequence & ownership

    Agree the recommended starting point, data call, stakeholders, decision gates, and next accountable owner.
