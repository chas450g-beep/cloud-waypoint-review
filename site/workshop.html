THE PURPOSE

Leave with a bounded next move.

The workshop is not a compressed assessment. It surfaces the decisions, evidence gaps, access constraints, and stakeholder responsibilities needed to scope the right work—whether that begins with Foundations, dependency mapping, or a coordinated program.

THREE HOURS · FOUR CONVERSATIONS

A practical agenda.

  1. 00–30

    Mission & outcomes

    Define the operational outcome, urgency, decision authority, and what success must enable.

  2. 30–75

    Cloud & boundary

    Review landing-zone status, classification boundaries, shared services, authorization posture, and ownership assumptions.

  3. 75–135

    Applications & evidence

    Examine inventories, application knowledge, telemetry, documentation, known dependencies, and blind spots.

  4. 135–180

    Sequence & ownership

    Agree the recommended starting point, data call, stakeholders, decision gates, and next accountable owner.

THE DECISION THE WORKSHOP RESOLVES

Where will this run? Three answers, and what each one costs you.

Most missions arrive assuming one of these and leave having chosen another. Three hours is usually enough to tell which one you are actually in — and many are in more than one at once.

Join an enterprise shared service

Fastest to a usable landing zone and the least to staff. You inherit the enterprise’s controls, and its constraints.

  • What the enterprise owns, and what still stays yours
  • Whether your data and access requirements fit inside their boundary
  • How an exception gets approved, and how long that takes
  • What happens when their roadmap and your mission diverge

Contract it out

Someone else stands it up, and may operate it. The question is what the contract actually buys.

  • Build, run, or both — and for how long
  • Who holds the authorization boundary while they hold the keys
  • Whether evidence comes back in a form you can reuse, or only as reports
  • What transfers to you at the end, and in what state

Stand it up internally

The most control and the most to staff. Viable when the mission already holds, or can hold, the skills.

  • The skills you must hold on day two, not on day one hundred
  • Who approves changes, and who approves exceptions to them
  • Whether shared services can be sustained, not just built
  • How long to a boundary an assessor would accept
