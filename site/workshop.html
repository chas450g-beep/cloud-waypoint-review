Who should attend and what to prepare

Include the mission sponsor, application owner, cybersecurity lead and hosting or operations representative. Add acquisition or funding participants when their decisions shape the next step.

Prepare an approved, non-sensitive summary of your goals, application landscape, known constraints and current security review path. Identify existing inventories, architecture, security plans and provider agreements that could inform a later data call. Agree secure handling before sharing any protected material.

Questions for DoD and disconnected missions
  • What connectivity must the mission operate with—or without?
  • Which enterprise services and existing designs may be reusable?
  • Where do application, data and organizational boundaries meet?
  • Who operates the environment and owns the authorization decisions?

Discuss the planning questions without introducing mission-sensitive information into a public channel.
