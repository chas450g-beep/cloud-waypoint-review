THE REBALANCE

Six weeks is a workstream—not the product.

Foundations is scoped at approximately six weeks; dependency mapping typically continues longer and overlaps after initial context is established. Together, they inform the destination design and prepare migration, security, authorization, and operating decisions earlier. Their value compounds when the two streams converge into one evidence-backed migration, security, and operating design.

A TYPICAL READINESS SHAPE
  1. 01

    Mobilize & collect

    Confirm objectives, stakeholders, environments, available documentation, inventories, and telemetry.

  2. 02

    Establish the foundation

    Define the GSS concept, boundary, shared services, target patterns, authorization inputs, and operating assumptions.

  3. 03

    Observe the estate

    Discover application communications, shared infrastructure, external services, data paths, and hidden coupling.

  4. 04

    Converge the evidence

    Reconcile stakeholder knowledge with observed behavior. Validate necessary interactions, record uncertainties, and propose migration groups and security policies for their different purposes.

  5. 05

    Prepare to launch

    Sequence waves, shape future-state data flows, prepare application ATO inputs, and establish implementation handoffs.

  6. 06

    Transition to operations

    Carry decisions, risks, owners, and evidence into GSS operations and continuous monitoring.
